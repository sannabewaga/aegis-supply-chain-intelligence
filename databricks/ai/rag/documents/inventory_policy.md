# AegisMart Inventory Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how inventory health should be monitored.

## Inventory Availability

Available inventory is defined as:

Available Quantity = Stock Quantity - Reserved Quantity

A stockout occurs when available inventory is less than or equal to zero.

## Inventory Monitoring

Inventory health should consider:

- Stock quantity.
- Reserved quantity.
- Available quantity.
- Stockout frequency.
- Demand indicators.
- Product category.
- Warehouse.
- Historical trends.

## Stockout Investigation

When stockouts increase, analysts should investigate:

1. Product demand.
2. Supplier performance.
3. Warehouse conditions.
4. Historical inventory levels.
5. Concentration of stockouts across products or locations.

## Supplier Relationship

Supplier deterioration may contribute to inventory problems, but inventory shortages should not automatically be attributed to supplier performance.

Demand changes and warehouse conditions should also be considered.

## Risk

Repeated stockouts affecting high-demand products should receive increased operational attention.
