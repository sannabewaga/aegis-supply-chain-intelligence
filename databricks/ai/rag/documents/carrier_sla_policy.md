# AegisMart Carrier SLA Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines expectations for logistics carriers handling AegisMart shipments.

## Carrier Performance

Carrier performance should be evaluated using:

- On-time delivery rate.
- Late-delivery rate.
- Average actual transit time.
- Average delivery delay.
- Transit-time variability.

## Investigation Threshold

A carrier should be investigated when:

- Late-delivery rate increases materially.
- Average delivery delay increases.
- Actual transit time consistently exceeds expected transit time.
- Transit-time variability increases.

## Context

Carrier performance can vary by warehouse, region, service type, and time period.

A carrier should therefore not be evaluated solely using an aggregate historical average.

## Root Cause Investigation

When a carrier deteriorates:

1. Compare its recent performance against historical performance.
2. Compare the carrier against other carriers.
3. Examine affected warehouses.
4. Examine affected regions.
5. Determine whether the deterioration is isolated or widespread.

## Operational Response

Persistent carrier deterioration should trigger operational review and consideration of alternative routing or carrier allocation where operationally feasible.
