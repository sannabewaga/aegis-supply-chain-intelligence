# AegisMart Warehouse Operations Policy

## Purpose

This document defines operational expectations for AegisMart fulfillment warehouses.

## Warehouse Performance

Warehouse performance should be evaluated using:

- Shipment throughput.
- Late-delivery rate.
- Average delivery delay.
- Inventory availability.
- Stockout rate.
- Processing efficiency.

## Bottleneck Investigation

When warehouse performance deteriorates, analysts should compare the affected warehouse against:

1. Its historical performance.
2. Other warehouses.
3. Shipment volume.
4. Carrier performance.
5. Inventory conditions.

High shipment volume alone does not establish that a warehouse is experiencing a bottleneck.

A bottleneck should be investigated when deterioration occurs alongside worsening processing or delivery indicators.

## Capacity

Warehouse capacity represents the nominal operational capacity associated with the warehouse.

Capacity should be considered together with actual shipment volume when evaluating potential congestion.

## Regional Effects

A warehouse may influence delivery performance for the regions it serves. Analysts should therefore examine geographic concentration when investigating warehouse-level problems.

## Recommended Investigation

For a warehouse with deteriorating performance:

1. Compare current and historical shipment metrics.
2. Examine late-delivery trends.
3. Examine average delivery delay.
4. Examine inventory availability and stockouts.
5. Examine carrier-level performance.
6. Compare against other warehouses.
