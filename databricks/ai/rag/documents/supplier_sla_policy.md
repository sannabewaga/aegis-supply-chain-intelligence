# AegisMart Supplier SLA Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the operational expectations for suppliers providing products to AegisMart.

## Supplier Delivery Standard

Suppliers are expected to maintain at least 95% on-time fulfillment performance.

A delivery is considered late when the actual delivery date exceeds the expected delivery date.

## Escalation Thresholds

Supplier performance should be reviewed when:

- On-time delivery falls below 95%.
- Late-delivery rate exceeds 5%.
- Average delivery delay exceeds 1 day.
- Performance deteriorates consistently across multiple reporting periods.

A supplier showing persistent deterioration should be investigated rather than evaluated solely on its historical average.

## Lead Time

Agreed supplier lead time represents the expected fulfillment duration for products supplied by the supplier.

Actual performance should be compared against agreed lead time when investigating supplier delays.

## Supplier Investigation

When investigating supplier performance, analysts should examine:

1. Historical delivery performance.
2. Change in late-delivery rate over time.
3. Average delivery delay.
4. Product mix.
5. Warehouse and carrier involvement.
6. Inventory consequences.

A deterioration should not automatically be attributed to the supplier without checking downstream logistics and inventory signals.

## Escalation

If supplier performance remains below SLA for multiple reporting periods, operations should investigate the affected products and determine whether alternative suppliers or mitigation actions are available.
